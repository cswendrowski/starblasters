## 3. Identified Problems

### P1 — Difficulty doesn't scale.
Production HP/damage unchanged across sectors. CLAUDE.md's promised multiplier doesn't exist. Players ramp power for free.

### P2 — Mk power asymmetry (weapons 9×, hull 1.8×).
Designer can't balance cannon vs hull — answer is always cannon. Hull's `max_hull = 20 if Mk≥9 else 10+Mk` has a suspicious Mk-9 cliff.

### P3 — Bosses are 72% of bounty.
Combat nodes earn ~3× less than bosses. Player can beeline bosses (POI-gated by row, but bounty incentive is missing).

### P4 — Mk 7-9 unreachable in 3 sectors.
Outpost cap = `sector + 3`. S3 cap = Mk 6, triangular bias toward midpoint. **Mk 9 unreachable without endless mode.** Most "Mk 9" balance numbers aren't realized in real runs.

### P5 — Repair / super refill too cheap.
30 bounty for +25% max hull. Full heal = 120 (half a Mk 4 upgrade). Outpost visit auto-refills supers for free, **and** a paid `SUPER_REFILL_COST=30` button exists — the paid one is dead UI.

### P6 — Resale arbitrage.
Outpost cannon sell-back: ~50%. Signal Event part sell: ~30%. Player picks better venue → no risk to over-purchase.

### P7 — Spread Cannon Mk-4 dead-zone.
+2 bullets/Mk caps at Mk 4 (9 bullets). Mk 5-9 just adds per-bullet damage — feels redundant.

### P8 — Missile/Rocket weapons currently free of ammo cost.
Upcoming "~60 ammo each" change has no economy hook yet. `Run.ammo` is MG-only.

### P9 — Sector modifiers underused as economy lever.
`wanted` (+20% bounty) is a nice mechanic but V3 map doesn't yet generate modifier tags.

### P10 — Free Smart Bomb auto-bombs on lethal hit + refills free per outpost.
Massive damage mitigation, zero economy cost.

## 4. Mk-Gating Proposal

**Recommendation: Asymmetric pillar caps + tier labels + boss-clear unlock bumps**

### (a) Decouple cannon Mk cap from upgrade Mk cap
- Upgrade cap = `min(9, 2 + sector_idx × 2)` → S1=4, S2=6, S3=8. Upgrades top out near Mk 8 in standard run.
- Cannon cap = `min(9, sector_idx × 3)` → S1=3, S2=6, S3=9. Cannons reach Mk 9 only by S3 — final cannon acquisition becomes the run's "build identity" moment.

### (b) Boss-clear unlock bumps
Each row-boss kill in a sector bumps the outpost cap floor for the remaining outposts. Rewards clearing the sector instead of skipping bosses.

### (c) Tier labels surface Mk
- Tier I — Salvage (Mk 1-3, green)
- Tier II — Standard (Mk 4-6, blue)
- Tier III — Prototype (Mk 7-9, orange) ← chase item

**Peer comparison:**
- Cobalt Core: act-gated card pool tiers.
- Steredenn: sector-gated weapon pool.
- Returnal: proficiency XP gates Tier-3 weapons (wallet-independent).

### Concurrent fix: flatten weapon power curve

Change `mark_multiplier()` from `float(mark)` to `1.0 + 0.25 × (mark - 1)` (Mk 1 = 1.0×, Mk 9 = 3.0×). Re-key each weapon's `base_damage` upward to keep Mk 1 viable (~3-4 dmg base for energy blaster). Hull grows: `max_hull = 10 + 2×hull_mk` → Mk 9 = 28 hull.

## 5. Other Observations + Designer Questions

### Stale CLAUDE.md
Delete the `× (1 + 0.05 × sectors_cleared)` claim or ship it. **Recommend ship it.** Even +5%/sector is only +15% by sector 3; consider +10%/sector.

### Combat-clear / boss-clear / hazard-clear bonuses
None exist. Consider:
- Combat clear: +20 bounty
- Hazard clear: +25 bounty (compensating mine/asteroid low payouts)
- Boss clear: already paid via `died.emit(bounty_value)`

### Sector modifiers as economy lever
When V3 wires modifier tags, **`wanted` POIs sell +30% bounty, `dangerous` POIs +50%** — actual risk-reward choice on the map.

### Hull formula Mk-9 cliff
`max_hull = 20 if Mk≥9 else 10 + Mk` → Mk 8 = 18, Mk 9 = 20 (the +2 cap jump is the largest delta). Recommend `10 + 2*hull_mk` (Mk 9 = 28) or `10 + 3*hull_mk` (Mk 9 = 37).

### Free super refill on outpost visit
`outpost.gd:69-71` auto-refills `super_charges = max_super_charges`. Paid 30-bounty button becomes dead UI. Pick one.

### Asteroid bounty = 0
Hazard nodes are bounty deserts (minefield = ~60 bounty for 60 mines = 1 each). Recommend default +1/asteroid, +2/mine, or a flat hazard-completion bonus.

### Per-sector outpost density (~3/sector)
Possibly too high. One outpost per sector + one Junk Trader signal might feel scarcer and more decisive.

### Hunter Drone bounty edge case
`enemy_roster.gd:97` lists 5 bounty; `enemy_hunter_drone.gd` says "no bounty for kamikaze hit" — verify the cancel-on-hit code path fires.
